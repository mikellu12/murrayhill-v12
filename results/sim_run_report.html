The equation

Section 2.7 of the manuscript. Multiplicative, not additive — if any dimension goes to zero the whole score collapses, which a weighted sum cannot express. The exponents are elasticities and sum to 1; they are not mixing weights.

SFV enters Y and D_raw both, as the manuscript specifies, which is why facade variation carries more influence than any other single input. Vnat/Vbuilt is a ratio of two raw ratings rather than a normalised one, so it spans 0.125 to 0.875 and cannot reach either end.

H/W, and how it got there

Ω discounts on the canyon aspect ratio and §2.8 picks the elasticity vector from it, so a half-view without H/W has no score at all. Two fixes today took coverage from 465 nodes to 715.

  • The width probe is a cone, not a line. A zero-width ray slides between footprint polygons and past building edges. The error being tolerated is angular — the street bearing is fitted, not known — so the probe widens with distance: 1.2 m across at 14 m, 7.9 m at 90 m. That recovered 36 nodes and left every per-street median intact (East 41st 19.0 m, Park Avenue 43.6 m, a 140 ft avenue).
  • Corner nodes inherit their street's H/W. 87% of nodes within 15 m of a crossing returned nothing, against a flat 12% elsewhere, because the perpendicular points down the cross street and there is no wall on it. That is undefined, not mismeasured, so those nodes take the median of measured nodes on the same street within 120 m. HW_source records which is which.
  • It validates. Measured H/W against measured enclosure (VEI) runs Spearman +0.532, p 5e-38, n 501 — up from +0.503 before the cone.
  • 30% of scored half-views carry an inherited value, not a measured one. That is an interim position, and it belongs in the methods rather than in a footnote.

Two things that do not work yet

  • Dependence is saturated. τD = 0.15 against a D_raw median of 0.444, so 76% of observations sit at the rail and D carries no information into M. The paper's thresholds were calibrated for pixel shares; normalised 1–7 ratings do not sit on that scale. Recentring on this study area's own median — which the manuscript explicitly sanctions — drops saturation to 29% and doubles D's spread. I is fine at 7%.
  • The typology contrast is absent, and slightly inverted. The paper reports mid-blocks at 0.78 against canyons at 0.22. We get canyons marginally above mid-blocks, either way round. The mechanism is the §2.8 regime shift: mid-blocks get a → 0.50 and canyons a → 0.30, so the regime leaning hardest on Imageability is applied exactly where Imageability is weakest — median I_raw is 0.111 because Murray Hill has almost no eye-level greenery anywhere.

Tuning exponents or thresholds until 0.78 / 0.22 appears is the trap CLAUDE.md names: do not fit a functional form because the previous one failed. Either the ratings are too flat to separate typologies here, or the paper's figures came from a pixel-share pipeline the VLM version will not reproduce, or it is a real negative result. That is a judgement about the study, not a parameter to search.
